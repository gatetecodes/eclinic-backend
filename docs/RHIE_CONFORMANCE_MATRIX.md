Status meanings:

- **Implemented**: admitted by the typed transport registry, response validated,
  and exposed through a tenant-scoped CareLogic workflow.
- **Gated**: transport support exists but invocation requires a disabled-by-default
  capability, audited correction workflow, or complete national mappings.
- **Externally blocked**: deliberately not callable because MoH rules or an
  authoritative external dependency are missing.

All POST and DELETE transport retries are disabled. Mutations retry only through
the durable outbox with deterministic UUIDv5 resource IDs and unique idempotency
keys. Empty 2xx bodies are accepted only where the pinned Swagger permits them.

## External go-live gates

UPID allocation, authoritative Facility/Practitioner/Coverage/NPC identifiers,
insurance behavior for self-pay, legal approval for consent and break glass,
secure production networking and credentials, rate limits/SLA/incident contacts,
profile and idempotency clarifications, DPIA approval, training, and production
acceptance remain external prerequisites and are surfaced in operations readiness.
